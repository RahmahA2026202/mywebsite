3. Shanay-timpishka (The Boiling River)

The Shanay-timpishka is a river located deep in the Peruvian Amazon rainforest near Mayantuyacu. In the local Asháninka language, Shanay-timpishka means "boiled with the heat of the sun". It is a 6.4 kilometre long thermal tributary, which frequently exceeds temperatures of 90°C and 100°C in its hottest sections. This river is known for killing anything that enters it. Scientists and geoscientists spent years trying to figure out why the river was like this. They then came to a conclusion; they said that despite it being located hundreds of miles away from the nearest active volcano, the water is heated by a deep geothermal system where rainwater seeps down through fault lines and is superheated by the Earth's crust before rising back up. Local indigenous communities consider the river sacred, and they believe that the boiling water is birthed by Yacumama, a giant serpent spirit known as the "Mother of the Waters".

4. Puerto Mosquito (Mosquito Bay)

Mosquito bay is the worlds brightest bioluminescent bay, and it is located on the southern shore of Vieques in Puerto Rico. This is a very rare natural phenomenon that occurs because the bay's pristine red mangrove tree ecosystem trapped high concentrations of Pyrodinium bahamense, a microscopic organism (dinoflagellate) that emits a brilliant neon blue-green flash of light when agitated by motion. You can book guided kayak tours with clear-bottoms to admire the waters! Independent exploration is strictly prohibited. It is most important to try and book tours as close to a New Moon as possible, as dark night skies will reveal the most vibrant and bright luminescence.
