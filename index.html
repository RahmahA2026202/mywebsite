Natural Beauties and Wonders of The World

This world is full of many beautiful natural wonders. Ranging from underwater phenomenons to eternal flames, there is so much to look at that we might miss.

In the next few pages, I will be inserting images and descriptions from different incredible places around the world which you might have not known existed. Prepare yourself to be wowed!

1. Aurora Borealis (Northern Lights):

This appears in the North sky and is only visible from the Northern Hemisphere (a loss for the Southern Hemisphere people). These beautiful, northern polar lights usually appear from September to October and March to April. The Aurora Borealis is named after the Roman goddess of dawn, Aurora and the Greek name for the north wind, Boreas. Often seen as a greenish glow or occasionally a faint red, the lights consist of solar wind and particles that appear as “curtains” or streamers extending in an east-west direction across the sky. Shaped by the earth’s magnetic field, the lights are constantly changing and evolving. The North American Indigenous people - formally know as "The Cree" - call this phenomenon the “Dance of the Spirits.” (Source: Victoria Falls Guide)

2. Great Barrier Reef

The Great Barrier Reef is located along the northeast coast of Queensland, Australia. It is the world's largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 kilometres. It comprises 2,900 individual reefs and 900 islands. It covers an area of approximately 344,400 square kilometres, and is larger than the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and the Netherlands combined (crazy, right?). The best time to visit is during the dry season, which is between June and October. The water clarity is at its best, there are calmer sea conditions and the risk of tropical cyclones and jellyfish blooms is at its lowest. (Source: Traveloka)
